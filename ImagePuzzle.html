How To Play

  • Goal: Reconstruct the scrambled image by sliding tiles into the open slot.
  • Multi-Tile Slide: Click any tile in the same row or column as the gap to slide the whole line at once!
  • Keyboard: Use Arrow Keys or W A S D to slide adjacent tiles.
  • Number Hints: Press Hints to show numbered badges (1, 2, 3...) on tile corners.
  • Custom Photos: Click Upload to solve your own pictures or wallpapers!
